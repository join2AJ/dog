    Food Guide 🍗

    Can my dog eat this? Search any food to find out.

    Feeding golden rules

    • Treats should make up no more than 10% of daily calories.
    • Switch foods gradually over 7–10 days to avoid an upset stomach.
    • Fresh, clean water must always be available.
    • Puppies need puppy food. Large-breed puppies need large-breed formulas.
    • If your dog eats something toxic, call your vet right away. Don't wait for symptoms.

    Adopting a Dog: Be a Great Dog Parent 🏡

    A dog is a 10–15 year commitment. Work through this checklist. Your progress is saved on this device.

    Adopt, don't shop? 💛

    Millions of healthy, loving dogs (including Indies) wait in shelters. Adopted dogs are often already vaccinated and sterilised. If you buy, choose a responsible breeder who health-tests the parents, lets you meet the mother, and never sells puppies younger than 8 weeks.
